# Privacy Policy

### HSmart ISP Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: November 2026**

**Harry Smart Technologies Ltd** ("HSmart", "we", "us", or "our") operates the **HSmart ISP** platform ([https://isp.hsmart.co.ke](https://isp.hsmart.co.ke)). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, and protection of information in compliance with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019**.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide automated ISP billing, hotspot management, and router telemetry, we collect:
1. **Tenant Account Information**: Your business name, contact person name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
2. **Network & Router Telemetry**: MikroTik router IP addresses, API port configurations, RouterOS versions, interface traffic, CPU/memory metrics, and online subscriber session logs.
3. **Billing & Transaction Data**: M-Pesa transaction reference codes, payment timestamps, amounts, subscriber phone numbers, and plan durations.
4. **Reseller & Agent Records**: Agent names, wallet balances, commission ledgers, and voucher distribution logs.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Authenticate and manage your ISP tenant account.
- Communicate with your MikroTik RouterOS devices to activate PPPoE and hotspot accounts upon payment.
- Reconcile M-Pesa C2B and STK Push payments.
- Dispatch expiry alerts, payment confirmations, and system notifications via SMS.
- Generate operational insights, monthly revenue summaries, and uptime health diagnostics.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Tenant Isolation
1. **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Each ISP's database records (customers, routers, transactions, and vouchers) are strictly isolated with dedicated tenant keys. One ISP cannot view or access another ISP's data.
2. **Credential Encryption**: MikroTik API passwords, M-Pesa consumer secrets, and webhook tokens are stored using AES-256 encryption.
3. **No Third-Party Data Selling**: We will never sell, lease, or distribute your subscriber lists or revenue data to third parties, advertisers, or competitors.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers
We only transmit data to third-party processors strictly required to execute core platform services:
- **Telecommunication Gateways**: Safaricom Daraja API for automated M-Pesa validation.
- **SMS Gateways**: Licensed Kenyan bulk SMS aggregators for delivery of voucher codes and payment receipts.
- **Infrastructure Providers**: Secure server hosting environments with automated encrypted offsite backups.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Subscriber Rights
1. **Subscriber Records**: Tenant customer records and transaction logs are maintained for the duration of your active subscription and up to statutory accounting retention periods.
2. **Right of Correction & Erasure**: Tenants may update, export, or delete subscriber records directly from their ISP management dashboard at any time.

---

### 6. Cookies & Session Storage
We use essential session cookies to authenticate administrators, persist dashboard sessions, and maintain security against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). We do not use third-party tracking or behavioral advertising cookies.

---

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect platform enhancements or regulatory amendments. Significant changes will be announced via your ISP portal dashboard or email notifications.
