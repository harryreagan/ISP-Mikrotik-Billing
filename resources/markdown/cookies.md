# Cookie Policy

### HSmart ISP Cookie Policy
**Last Updated: November 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how **Harry Smart Technologies Ltd** ("HSmart", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on the **HSmart ISP** platform located at [https://isp.hsmart.co.ke](https://isp.hsmart.co.ke) (the "Service", "Platform").

---

### 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you access a website or web application. They are widely used to ensure websites function properly, maintain authenticated sessions, enhance security, and remember your preferences across navigation steps.

---

### 2. How We Use Cookies
HSmart ISP uses cookies strictly for **essential operational purposes**:
- **Authentication & Session State**: Keeping your administrator or subscriber account logged in as you navigate between dashboard modules, router monitoring tabs, and customer profiles.
- **CSRF Token Security**: Protecting forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery (`XSRF-TOKEN`) attacks.
- **Preferences**: Storing your interface preferences such as collapsed sidebar state, language choice (English / Swahili), and dark/light color themes.
- **Captive Portal Handshakes**: Temporarily storing router challenge tokens during hotspot voucher or M-Pesa STK login verification.

---

### 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `isp_billing_session` | Encrypted session state to maintain your login status | 2 Hours / Session | Essential |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | Cryptographic token to verify authenticity of POST requests | 2 Hours | Security |
| `hsmart_lang` | Remembers your preferred interface language (EN/SW) | 1 Year | Functional |
| `hsmart_theme` | Remembers light / dark display preference | 1 Year | Functional |

---

### 4. Third-Party and Advertising Cookies
**We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or data-broker trackers.** Your browsing history and network configuration data are never monetized, tracked across external sites, or shared with commercial advertising networks.

---

### 5. Managing and Disabling Cookies
You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling **essential session cookies** (`isp_billing_session`, `XSRF-TOKEN`) will prevent you from signing in to the ISP dashboard or completing subscriber voucher logins.

---

### 6. Updates to This Cookie Policy
We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory adjustments. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
